# v0.81 QA: Level 20 (Tall Grass Field), world-map node 10, first playable
- New world-map node after Flooded Castle (clear it to unlock; the level-select ROM opens it). The original path bends once between the nodes.
- The whole route is there: 6450 x 448 scene baked from the original (beach with the boat, the plain with the bear huts, the fortress with the ladder and the top deck), collision converted from BSP20 (two walkable bands at the fortress), 22 waypoints.
- Encounters at the original waypoints: 4 hut bears (WP4), the bears from the right (WP6), the boss (WP7) with the reinforcement stream, the propeller bears (WP9), the bears from the left (WP14), seven bats (WP20), then the exit door at the end of the top deck.
- The ladder (up at the foot, down at the top) is a scripted 40 tick climb.
- Music: NG90532 for the level and DRUMLIFT for the boss, then the usual victory track and score tally.
- Stand-ins (everything below is still to do): the bears, the boss and the bats use the shared barbarian enemy, not the bear knights; the propeller entry, magic, sword-lock and the bats' flight are not ported; the boss walk-in clip, the hut scene, the end cinematic (sprite 244) and the cannon clips are not ported; no pickups (weapons 54, 55, 62 need their original names); the sky is a plain gradient, the original parallax layers are not ported; animated scenery is frozen on its first frame.
- The level name is unconfirmed.
- Not run on hardware.
