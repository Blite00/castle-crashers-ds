# Barbarian Boss source art and DS budget

The LEVEL7 scenery uses the original game sprite 290 at half scale, with source origin (-768,-700), a 1024x448 DS canvas, and the original BSP7 corridor. Runtime-only guide clips, dynamic chest and princess clips are removed from the terrain. Registered gate and palisade foreground layers preserve the original placement depths. No new full-screen decode buffer is required: terrain remains 32x64 independently compressed row chunks and foreground pieces fit the existing 14,336-byte scratch buffer.

`boss_actor_art.h` holds original EBARBOSS and LEVEL7 art in 32x64 independently decoded transparent tiles. All tiles are bounded at 2,048 bytes. `boss_actor_frame_for` selects authored 30 Hz timeline keys; walk repeats at 36 ticks, other timelines hold their final key. Rendering should skip offscreen tiles before decoding and cache recently used tiles. The maximum boss silhouette is substantially larger during authored slam debris than during idle; a full silhouette cache is therefore inappropriate.

## Knight kiss color preservation

The eight source keys per knight are frames1,33,65,69,73,77,81,85. The actual kiss loops source65–86. The global actor palette reserves the four original knight helmet base colors and their four source highlights; its other55 entries remain adaptive. Without these reserved entries the much larger brown boss artwork can consume the palette and turn a green helmet gray. `boss_actor_art_test.c` now checks that every rendered time tick0–85 for every selected knight contains at least40 pixels of that knight's exact RGB555 source helmet color, as well as the existing full tile hashes. The fix changes no poses, source timing, buffer sizes, or tile dimensions.
