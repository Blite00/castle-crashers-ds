## v0.39 boss combat corrections (2026-10-05)

The shield slam now buries a grounded knight at its frame37/38 body contact rather than always launching them. EBARBOSS sprite120 enables `smasher`; MAIN `f_CheckPushCharOut` sets `burried` and `nohit` on humanoid contact. The explicit slam power30 receives the existing campaign Defense scaling. Jumping above the contact height avoids burial.

Original PLAYER sprite1680 supplies the escape sequence: entry frames1–29, then RIGHT/LEFT/RIGHT/LEFT/RIGHT/LEFT/RIGHT/LEFT at frames30–37; frame38 resumes playback and frame41 clears burial/immunity. The DS implementation preserves that horizontal sequence, rejects both directions held together, blocks movement/attacks while trapped, and uses four emergence ticks followed by a small escape hop and24 ticks of recovery immunity. The exact eight alternations and entry timing come from the source; the hop/recovery window and simplified rectangular slam contact are adaptations. The bottom screen prompts the affected local player. Boss death and replay clear the state.

Progress occupies existing boss-only prop slots14/15. Hero snapshot cooldown bit64 marks the state, and its animation byte carries source frame1–41, including entry and emergence; malformed frames/phase combinations are rejected. No packet or persistent structure grows. The same state renders on host and guest.

LEVEL7 `f_SpawnBarbarianBoss` sets `damage_all=true`, and MAIN `f_EnemyAttack` checks the enemy array in that case. Punch and reverse-spike contact now also damage the boss's Barbarian reinforcements. Defeated reinforcements remain physical corpses and use normal independent blink/drop timing. Boss hit feedback does not cancel its attacks or add ordinary humanoid hitstun.

Humanoid campaign sword locks now use the existing arena clash counters. Every third eligible simultaneous grounded trade involving a heavy attack can lock a hero with an enemy; ordinary trades still deal damage. A/X/B taps compete against a paced NPC, with the existing five-point lead/ten-tap/90-tick resolution and180-tick cooldown. Other actors continue simulating. The giant boss is not a sword-lock participant. These timing/selection rules are explicit DS adaptations; the original source supplies the button-mash concept and lead/score thresholds. Boss-ending player-versus-player keeps its separate existing duel behavior. Guest snapshots carry the clashing actor poses and both scores without extra wire bytes.
