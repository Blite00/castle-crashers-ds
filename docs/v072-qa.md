# v0.72 QA: Mill Run fixes and cut grass
- Monster arms: both claws (sprites 189 and 193) now animate through their 47-frame loops (open, slam, rake), every sixth frame kept, instead of one still frame.
- Chase speed: the DS view shows 288 world pixels where the original showed 424, so source speeds crossed 1.47 times more of the screen per tick. Guide, log and finale speeds are scaled by 17/25 (logs 98/16 px per tick, guide up to 109/16). The obstacle waves, mash line and the pattern are unchanged; the chase is about 1.5 times longer.
- Doorways: the translucent light on the floor in front of each wall kind (original sprite 132 frames 10 to 12) is back: it is stored as a flat shape and blended at 7/16, so the lane to aim for shows long before the wall arrives. Before, the alpha cut dropped it.
- Cut grass (Forest Entrance, Thieves' Forest): the ground patch over a cut tuft is now painted before any actor is drawn, so heroes and enemies standing there are never covered by a ground-coloured silhouette, and the patch copies the dirt from beside the tuft on the same rows instead of one stretched column.
- Deer bank: 839 KB plus 192 KB music (3.2 kHz) fits 1,040 KB; the victory clip for this bank is 5.8 kHz. Not run on hardware.
