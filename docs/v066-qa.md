# v0.66 QA: monster eye and arm, score tally
- Mill Run: the entrance wall (sprite 208) now wobbles from frame 242 and breaks at 285, the monster's eye, teeth and the long arm (sprite 194, from frame 316) are drawn, and the entrance camera pans to the monster and back to the jump like the source's cinematic camera. The monster's other arm (sprite 190) is empty in every frame and is not drawn. The pooping deer gag and the DeerTrainer achievement are still not ported.
- Score tally after Forest Entrance, Thieves' Forest and Mill Run (MAIN f_ScoreTally, HUD sprite 63): the wooden sign with skull, gold bag and XP drops in beside each player, kills, gold and experience gained in the level count up one after the other (frames 17, 25, 33 of the clip) and the party leaves for the world map after 170 ticks, or one second after any A press. The sign art is the original; the numbers use the game's menu font, not the original number font.
- Music: Waterflame_RaceVictory (MAIN music slot 4) plays during the tally. Forest and Thieves use the existing victory clip; Mill Run uses a 6.5 kHz copy because its bank has less room.
- Kills are attributed to the hero who landed the hit (or the nearer hero when a pet or arrow killed). Guests receive each hero's kills in the idle kill and gate counters of the completion snapshot; gold and experience come from the profile difference to the level's first snapshot.
- Not covered: the war and boss levels (they hand over to the next level without a sign), the hardware.
- Memory: margin beyond reserves is about 51 KB.
