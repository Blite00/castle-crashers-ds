# v0.65 QA: Mill Run (LEVEL22), deer ride and the Mill Monster
- New world-map node after Thieves' Forest (original node 22). Two players only; the source's four heroes and four deer are cut to two.
- Intro (entrance clip, 316 ticks): monster shakes, explosions and roars at the source frames, deer take fright at 280, heroes jump on at 305, music starts at frame 285 as in sprite 188.
- Chase: the deer run on their own; the camera rides the guide (speed 1 to 20 source units, ramp 190 ticks). 21 log waves from sprite 141: piles are jumped, walls must be passed through their gap lane. A slam pins the rider at the log face; falling behind the guide by 37 pixels triggers the monster's toss (10 damage, 24 ticks). A log that reaches the monster explodes and sets riders in front of it back.
- Finale: the flume breaks up, the guide runs to the dock, both heroes dock-jump and the level completes (clear bit 64).
- Art: the original mill (sprite 88) laid out as one flume with one extra copy of the loop tile so the chase can wrap without a seam; deer from mount3.pak; riders for the four knights from PLAYER deer_walk, deertoss, deerslam, jumptohorse and dockjump clips; monster, logs and walls from LEVEL22. Everything is in chapter bank 7 (deer.bin, 786 KB) with the level music (NG36657, 240 KB) in its tail.
- Everything that moves on its own is a pure function of the timer, so the guest renders the level from the 276-byte snapshot with no new wire fields.
- Tests: tests/campaign_deer_test.c (timeline, deterministic tables, bots that dodge and bots that idle, wire validity every 5 ticks, world-map unlock), tests/level_contract_test.c row, tools/level_audit.py.
- Limits: the entrance overlay clip (eye in the wall, sprite 208), the monster's arms (190 and 194), the pooping deer gag and the DeerTrainer achievement are not ported. Ground2 and Ground3 sounds are best-guess files. Log hit zones are approximated from the source markers (the hitzone shape was not decodable), jump height and the push-back after a slam are tuned by test, not by source. The chase speed matches the source numbers but the visual scroll is the flume moving under a camera, not the source's sliding mill clip. Not run on DS hardware.
- Memory: scratch grew by 40 KB for the monster frames; margin beyond reserves is 60,620 bytes.
