# v0.75 QA: Flooded Castle (LEVEL23), world-map node 9
- New world-map node after Mill Run (clear Mill Run to unlock it; the level-select ROM opens it directly). The map window now scrolls vertically to fit the extra route.
- Swim down the flooded corridor with the original timeline (sprite 540): food drops, shark fins (single, pair, three, diagonal sets), rocks every 30 to 120 ticks that knock swimmers off, bats from the right and left edge, four fat-fish checkpoints that wait until every enemy is dead, then the Catfish boss (600 health, 900 with two players). The Catfish shrugs off three quarters of any hit that does not land while it is recovering.
- Rocks and fins are pure functions of the level timer, so the second player sees them without extra network data. Hazards hit once as they pass a swimmer.
- Art baked from the original clips: swimming poses for all four knights, fat fish, bats, rocks, fins, Catfish (idle, three punches, death) and the king boat. Music 007_FLOODED_CASTLE, boss music 015_CORN_BOSS, victory Waterflame_RaceVictory, then the score tally.
- Not done yet (known gaps against the original): the riding logs, the grab by bats ("bathead" pose is baked but not used), the fat fish charge after five hits, the Catfish mode machine and resistances, the king boat attack, the intro and ending clips, ground and water sound effects. Enemies currently use the standard enemy AI in place of the original swim AI.
- Not run on hardware.
