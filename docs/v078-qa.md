# v0.78 QA: Flooded Castle enemies follow the original scripts
- All three enemy kinds now think for themselves instead of using the shared enemy AI (they carry knight_id 2, which keeps that AI off): campaign_flood.c.
- Fat fish: walk to their prey at source speed 4, leap (30 tick jump clip, 12 damage) when they touch a swimmer, and charge in a straight line on the fifth hit they take. They spawn at the rock spawner (the second of the last pair from the left edge).
- Bats: fly to a point near a random swimmer at a random height and carry off a swimmer who stands still (sprite 1737 "bathead"): the swimmer cannot move or be hit, takes 5 damage every 30 ticks, and six button presses throw the bat off. Bats now spawn at the screen edges (right with the original random spread, left for the left-edge ones).
- Catfish (sprite 298 f_Boss): wait, pick a swimmer, chase to 100 pixels behind it and punch, then spit a hairball at the king boat. When the hairball reaches the boat the cannon fires along the boat's lane and stuns the Catfish for 120 ticks if it stands in that lane; only a stunned Catfish takes full damage, otherwise a quarter. Below half health it moves out of the lane and charges across the corridor up to five passes, 10 damage and a knock-off; ramming the boat costs it 10 health. The boss meter on the bottom screen shows its health.
- Shark fins use the original table (14 spawns with their x and y offsets, from the right or from the left), slide in, pause ten ticks, then accelerate. Rocks only hit swimmers on the surface (not jumping) within 50 pixels in front of them and 5 pixels off their lane, and push the swimmer out of the rock's lane.
- Knights drop in from above at the start.
- Art added: hairball, cannon ball, king boat firing frame.
- Not done: riding the logs, the intro and ending clips, ground and water sounds. Enemy speeds and sizes are source/2 scaled by 17/25 as everywhere else.
- Not run on hardware.
