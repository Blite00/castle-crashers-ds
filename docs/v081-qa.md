# v0.81 QA: Level 20 (Tall Grass Field), world-map node 10, first playable
- New world-map node after Flooded Castle (clear it to unlock; the level-select ROM opens it). The original path bends once between the nodes.
- The whole route is there: 6450 x 448 scene baked from the original (beach with the boat, the plain with the bear huts, the fortress with the ladder and the top deck), collision converted from BSP20 (two walkable bands at the fortress), 22 waypoints.
- Encounters at the original waypoints: 4 hut bears (WP4), the bears from the right (WP6), the boss (WP7) with the reinforcement stream, the propeller bears (WP9), the bears from the left (WP14), seven bats (WP20), then the exit door at the end of the top deck.
- The ladder is a walkable shaft: up and down climb it (a scripted 40 tick climb before v0.84 produced positions the guest rejected).
- Music: NG90532 for the level and DRUMLIFT for the boss, then the usual victory track and score tally.
- Stand-ins (everything below is still to do): the bears, the boss and the bats use the shared barbarian enemy, not the bear knights; the propeller entry, magic, sword-lock and the bats' flight are not ported; the boss walk-in clip, the hut scene, the end cinematic (sprite 244) and the cannon clips are not ported; no pickups (weapons 54, 55, 62 need their original names); the sky is a plain gradient, the original parallax layers are not ported; animated scenery is frozen on its first frame.
- The level name is unconfirmed.
- Not run on hardware.

## v0.82 additions
- Level name confirmed as Tall Grass Field (Castle Crashers wiki: the bear boss, Rammy after it, Hawkster found with the horn outside the huts, Burly Bear behind a bombable wall).
- The bears, the boss and the bats now wear the original bear knight costume (PLAYER helmet, emblem and shield 20, weapon 29, the fish), baked into the level bank as enemy species 4 on the thief pose layout.

## v0.83 additions
- Bats (WP20) are their own enemies now: seven bats fly a straight line left at 7 to 9 source units a tick at height 25, hurt a knight they touch (6) and leave the screen (f_BatFlyLeft), drawn from the original bat clip.
- The beefy bears at WP9 enter on propellers (80 and 60 ticks): they are tossed across and drop from the top of the screen to the ground, drawn from the original propeller clip in the bear costume (health 100, 120 with two players).
- The boss: health 190 (275 with two players: source 100 + 100 for f_BadDude + 150 per player, at the 0.55 scale used for the other bears), invulnerable and dancing (the dance3 clip in the bear costume) for the first 120 ticks while both knights walk to their marks p*_4, with the leash opened to WP7 + 300 as in the source; then the stream of reinforcements (fewer than four enemies alive: one more bear every 45 ticks).
- Tests: propeller descent (80 to the ground), bat flight height and exit, boss dance and invulnerability, the party reaching its marks.
